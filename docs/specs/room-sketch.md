# Room sketch to scale drawing

Agreed with James, Nov 2026. A new tool in the Drawings tab: sketch a room by hand, get a tidy measured plan, and turn it into a true scale drawing when needed.

## Drawing the room
- Sketch the room roughly with a finger or pen. On lift-off the wobbly lines become straight walls and the corners are squared to 90°; it snaps to 45° only where one was clearly meant (a bay or a cut corner).
- To change a wall: drag its corner, or tap the wall and type its real length (and angle). The rest of the room adjusts to match.
- Every wall shows its length in mm along the outside.

## Chimney breasts and boxings
- Tap a wall or corner and choose chimney breast, boxing, pier or alcove, then type its width and depth.
- It cuts into the wall line automatically and labels each new face.
- Each wall shows two rows of measurements:
  - a breakdown row, e.g. `alcove 950 · chimney 1,400 · alcove 900`;
  - an overall row with the full wall length, e.g. `3,250`, so the total is never lost.

## Measurements that don't add up
The sketch is not to scale, so real sizes come from what is typed (e.g. from a laser measure). Real rooms are rarely square: if the measurements don't close up, show the gap (e.g. "out by 15 mm") and let James choose which corner takes it up. Never force the room square and hide the difference.

## Not to scale / Make to scale
- **Sketch mode**: a clear "NOT TO SCALE" label, on screen and on every PDF or print. Measurements are correct; the shape is only roughly right.
- **"Make to scale" button**: redraws the room exactly from the typed measurements.
  - Scales: 1:1, 1:2, 1:5, 1:10, 1:20, 1:25, 1:50, 1:100, 1:200, 1:500.
  - Paper: A4, A3, A2, A1, A0, portrait or landscape.
  - **Best fit**: the largest standard scale that fits the room on the chosen paper. Warns when a choice won't fit, e.g. "too big for A4 at 1:20, try A3 or 1:50".
  - The note becomes e.g. "Scale 1:50 @ A4", and a scale bar is added so the print can be measured with a ruler.
  - Walls without a measurement: say which ones before converting.
  - Measurements that don't close: show the gap first so James decides where it goes.
- **Back to sketch** at any time. Both stay linked: changing a measurement updates the scale drawing.
- The PDF is exactly the true paper size, so A1/A0 print to scale on a plotter or at a print shop. For anything bigger than A4 it can also print on A4 with a "reduced – not to scale" note, for a site copy.
